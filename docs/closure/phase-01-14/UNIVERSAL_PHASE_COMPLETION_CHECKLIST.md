Apply this checklist to every phase.

## Status Definitions

FULLY CLOSED:
All required gates are passed and evidence is archived.

FUNCTIONALLY COMPLETE / PENDING FORMAL CLOSURE:
Work appears complete, but one or more evidence items are missing.

LOCKED:
Phase cannot proceed or be closed until an explicit unlock decision is made.

INCOMPLETE:
Work remains outstanding.

DEFERRED:
Known item intentionally moved to a later phase with owner approval.

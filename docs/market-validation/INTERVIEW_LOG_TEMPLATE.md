# Market Validation Interview Log

Target:
50 Malaysian legal-market conversations.

Interview Groups:
- Sole proprietor lawyers
- Litigation clerks
- Small firm partners
- Chambering students
- Legal secretaries
- Ex-law firm staff
- Law lecturers

Questions:
1. How do you track court dates?
2. How do you track filing deadlines?
3. What gets missed?
4. What is still done in Excel/WhatsApp/manual files?
5. What would you pay to fix?
6. What software do you already use?
7. What would make you reject new software?
